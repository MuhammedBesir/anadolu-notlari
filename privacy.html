Anadolu Notları

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

1. Summary

"Anadolu Notları Uploader" (the "App") is used only by the channel owner to upload the owner's own videos. It does not collect data from other people.

2. Data the App handles

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, granted by the owner.
  • Basic profile info of the owner's account (e.g. open ID, display name) returned by the platform during sign-in.
  • The video files and captions the owner chooses to publish.

3. How it is used

Tokens are used only to publish the owner's videos and check upload status. Nothing is used for advertising, analytics or profiling.

4. Storage

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer, in files readable only by the owner. They are not sent to any server other than the official platform APIs.

5. Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

6. Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from the platform's settings (e.g. TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions); local token files are then deleted.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children and does not knowingly process children's data.

8. Contact

Via the channel: youtube.com/@AnadolununNotlari

Türkçe özet

Araç yalnızca kanal sahibinin kendi hesap izinlerini, kendi bilgisayarında saklar ve sadece video yüklemek için kullanır. Başka kişilerin verisi toplanmaz, kimseyle paylaşılmaz.
